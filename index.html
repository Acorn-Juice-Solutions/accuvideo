How it works

  1. 1

    Point AccuVideo at your videos

    Pick one or more folders on your machine. AccuVideo handles MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WMV, FLV, WebM, M4V, TS, MPEG.

  2. 2

    Index locally

    Transcripts, on-screen text, and visual descriptions are computed on your machine. Embeddings go into a vector index — your raw video never leaves.

  3. 3

    Ask, and land on the second

    Type a question. Pick the result. The player opens at the exact frame that answers it — with seek-back controls if you want context.

Three ways to add videos

Pick whichever fits how your library is organised. You can mix and match — folders for the big archive, drag-and-drop for one-off files, and the headless CLI for overnight batches.

📁

Select a folder

Point AccuVideo at one or more folders on your disk.

  • Videos are indexed in place — nothing is copied or moved.
  • Zero extra disk usage beyond the index itself.
  • Ideal for organised libraries and large archives on internal drives, NAS or external disks.
✥

Drag & drop videos

Drop individual files straight into the app window.

  • Files are copied into a managed temporary folder — your video is duplicated on disk.
  • Convenient when files are scattered, on removable media, or you just want to try the app on a single clip.
  • Heads-up: large drops can grow that managed folder significantly.

💡 You can reclaim that space any time from Settings → Storage: clear the temporary copies without losing your index or search results.

Batch CLI — AccuVideoIngest

Headless command-line tool for unattended batch ingestion of entire archives.

AccuVideoIngest.exe ingest "K:/MyVideos"
  • Recursively scans a folder for mp4, mkv, avi, mov, wmv, flv, webm, m4v, ts, mpeg and indexes everything it finds.
  • Two subcommands: ingest (transcribe + embed + upload to your vector index) and transcribe (transcripts to JSON only).
  • Tunable per run: --model, --language, --gap, --max-seconds, --min-chars, --mode (audio / visual / both) and --force to re-transcribe.
  • Reads VIDEO_FOLDER from the environment when no path is passed — perfect for Windows Task Scheduler overnight jobs.
  • Progress logs and resume-on-failure built in: re-runs skip what's already indexed.

💡 Ships with the Pro installer under <apps_dir>/AccuVideo/AccuVideoIngest/. Run AccuVideoIngest --help for the full option list.

100% local. Your videos never leave your machine.

No cloud upload of your content. No subscription to read your own archive. Indexing, transcription, vision and search all run on your hardware, with optional GPU processing on compatible NVIDIA systems.

🖥

Videos stay on your disk

The actual MP4, MKV and friends never get uploaded anywhere. Transcription, on-screen text reading and visual analysis all run on your own CPU/GPU — no cloud step, no third-party server seeing your footage.

📍

Only abstract points reach the index

What goes into the vector database are embeddings — high-dimensional numerical points derived from your content. They power the semantic search but can't be reversed into watchable video, readable transcripts or recognisable frames.

🔒

Your collection, only yours

Every user gets a fully isolated collection, keyed to their license. No other AccuVideo user can query your library, see your snippets, or even know that your collection exists. Multi-tenant by design, single-tenant in practice.

System requirements

AccuVideo Basic runs on commodity laptops. Pro adds visual ingestion (Florence-2). Both editions now support optional NVIDIA CUDA acceleration on Windows — runs on CPU by default, opt into GPU if a compatible card is detected.

Basic — audio + on-screen text

  • CPU: Intel i5 (8th gen) / Ryzen 5 2600+ / Apple Silicon M1
  • RAM: 8 GB
  • GPU: integrated is enough. Optional CUDA acceleration: if an NVIDIA GPU is detected, Basic offers to enable GPU mode and downloads a small bundled DLL pack (.zip) on demand.
  • Disk: SSD with ~50 GB free
  • OS: Windows 10/11, macOS 12+

Pro — adds visual ingestion

  • CPU: Intel i7 (10th gen+) / Ryzen 7+ / Apple Silicon M2+
  • RAM: 16 GB (32 GB for batch / multi-client)
  • GPU: optional but strongly recommended for visual indexing — NVIDIA GPU with ≥6 GB VRAM and CUDA support. The CUDA build of Pro ships as a separate .zip (>2 GB) hosted externally; the download link and SHA-256 hash are published next to each GitHub release.
  • Disk: SSD for OS + models; HDD or SSD for the video library
  • Network: stable connection for the managed vector DB

Full hardware notes, multi-client setup and ingestion timing benchmarks: see the hardware requirements doc.
