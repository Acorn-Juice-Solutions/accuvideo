Three ways to find the moment

AccuVideo indexes every layer of your videos and lets you search across them in plain language.

🎙

Spoken words

Whisper transcribes every video locally. Search the dialogue, the lecture, the meeting — and jump to the second it was said.

🔤

On-screen text

Slides, captions, signs, code on a whiteboard. AccuVideo reads what's printed on screen and makes it searchable.

👁

Visual content

A vision-language model describes what's actually in the frame — "kids blowing out candles", "dance scene", "whiteboard formula".

How it works

  1. 1

    Point AccuVideo at your videos

    Pick one or more folders on your machine. AccuVideo handles MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WMV, FLV, WebM, M4V, TS, MPEG.

  2. 2

    Index locally

    Transcripts, on-screen text, and visual descriptions are computed on your machine. Embeddings go into a vector index — your raw video never leaves.

  3. 3

    Ask, and land on the second

    Type a question. Pick the result. The player opens at the exact frame that answers it — with seek-back controls if you want context.

System requirements

AccuVideo runs on commodity laptops. Visual indexing benefits from a recent GPU.

Minimum

  • CPU: Intel i5 (8th gen) / Ryzen 5 2600+
  • RAM: 8 GB
  • GPU: integrated
  • Disk: SSD ~50 GB free
  • OS: Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Ubuntu 20.04+

Recommended

  • CPU: Intel i7 (10th gen+) / Ryzen 7+ / Apple Silicon M2+
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti+ for visual ingestion
  • Disk: NVMe SSD 500 GB+ for the video library
  • Network: stable connection for the managed vector DB
